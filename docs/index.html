About

notes on the subject

I'm a Systems Engineer on Snap's Enterprise Engineering team in Palo Alto. I like the unglamorous, high-leverage work: replacing spreadsheets and access workarounds with auditable apps, and turning a busy support queue into an AI assistant people actually trust.

Right now I'm the sole engineer (and PM) on Snap's IT Budget Portal and tech lead on SnappyBot, a multi-agent IT-support assistant built on Vertex AI and Gemini. Before Snap, I built cloud pipelines for genomic sequencing data at a biotech startup, and worked on identity and accessibility at Expedia Group.

I studied Computer Science and Molecular & Cell Biology at UC Berkeley, which is why this page looks like a lab notebook.
